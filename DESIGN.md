# Design System: Talk to Uzair (five themes, one call)

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Hear the Call, Then See the Wait"**

One call model wears five complete visual worlds. **Horizon** (the default since it shipped) is not a dashboard: it is a full-screen night world drawn in WebGL, in Hoplon's black and lime. A dark mirror orb floats over a mirror sea; the orb's edge is a ring of 180 thin bars that move with whoever is speaking (lime for the agent, a cool grey for the Lead). The scenery changes in six chapters as the call moves through its stages: night sea, mountains with lime contour lines rising out of the water, a pass under the surface, an aurora, first light, and a lime dawn seam on the horizon at the close. The agent's words appear as large centred captions, revealed word by word. Horizon shows no timing at all: the call is the show. It opens on an entry card (name, "Who are you today?", Start the call, or hear a recorded sample first) and ends on a close card with the booking and a three-line recap.

The other four are the measurement themes. Studio is a dark recording session: a transport bar with an LCD clock, stacked lanes for Time, Stages, Lead, Omar, Wait and Events, and an Inspector, Wait editor and Code state below. Console (the default from 2026-10-07 until Horizon) wears the hoplonco.com identity: black, white and one electric lime, Epilogue, pill buttons and the real wordmark. A black call deck carries the Lead, the recording (Lead in grey, the agent in lime), a six-step lime stage track and the measured waits; it shows the stage while the call runs and the outcome when it ends. Below it, a white transcript puts a verdict badge on each reply, and a tabbed side panel opens any reply as a large readout. Enterprise is a premium workspace: a Hoplon navy band with a gold primary action, a six-step stepper, raised KPI tiles that overlap the band, and soft-shadowed cards. Showcase is a navy landing page where the working call window is the product shot, followed by long editorial sections.

The four measurement themes share no surface, font or radius. They share the measurement. Each shows the gap between the Lead's last word and the agent's first sound as one bar split into six parts in a fixed order, and judges totals against the same two targets (0.9 s typical, 1.5 s slow). Horizon shares Console's brand (the hoplonco.com black and lime, Epilogue) but none of its measurement. The theme switch is a segmented control inside each theme's own chrome (in Horizon, behind a small "Theme" disclosure at the bottom right); the choice is saved per browser (`localStorage` key `omar-ui`) and in the URL (`?ui=horizon|studio|console|enterprise|showcase`), with the URL winning on load. The sample call is labelled synthetic in every theme. The agent's name (Nimra or Uzair) comes from the chosen voice at runtime; lane and token names in the older themes keep `omar`, the agent's first name.

Density follows the world: Horizon is one fixed full screen with no scroll and very few words over the scene; Studio (13.5px) and Console (14px) are tight tools that fill one viewport (`100dvh`, internal scroll), Enterprise is a roomier 14px page with a 1400px content column, and Showcase is a 16px scrolling page with section padding up to 7rem. The old house look (Noto Sans, navy-band ledger) is retired; the Hoplon logo and name stay.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Five themes, one call model; Horizon is the default; theme switch saved per browser and via `?ui=`.
- Horizon: black dominates, lime only on lines, edges and the one filled action; the voice drives the world; no numbers.
- Six part inks per measurement theme, always in the order Turn detect, Commit, Tool step, LLM first token, Guard, Voice (TTS).
- Green, amber and red read as verdicts against 0.9 s and 1.5 s (measurement themes only).
- Numbers use tabular, lining figures everywhere (`tnum`, `lnum` on the shared `.ui` root).
- Voices only: no music, chimes or generated sound. Horizon's sample is a recorded call with synthetic voices; the measurement themes speak theirs with browser voices; both are labelled synthetic.
- One SVG icon family (24px grid, 1.75 stroke, round caps) sized 16px by default.

## Colors

Each measurement theme owns a complete palette under `.ui-<theme>`, set through the same variable names (`--bg`, `--sf`, `--line`, `--tx`/`--tx2`/`--tx3`, `--ac`, `--good`/`--warn`/`--crit`, `--p-*`), so shared components restyle themselves. Horizon has its own small set (`--hz-black`, `--hz-lime`, `--hz-grey`, `--hz-white`, `--hz-line`) because it uses no shared blocks except the theme switch.

### Primary
- **Horizon Lime** (horizon-lime, the hoplonco.com lime): a line and edge colour. It draws the orb's equaliser bars, the faint ring behind them and the orb rim, the mountain contour lines, the dawn seam and the bloom around them; in the chrome it marks the agent's caption label, the words said with feeling (lime italics), the chapter numeral, the "speaking" dot, the select chevron, focus rings and text selection. Its only fills are the one primary button and the selected theme option.
- **Session Amber** (studio-omar): Omar's voice in Studio and Studio's accent. It fills Omar's lane regions, the Call button, the selected Lead option and the text-selection tint.
- **Hoplon Lime** (console-lime, from hoplonco.com): Console's brand signal, used as a fill or on black only, never as text on white. The live status pill, the won outcome, the stage track, the agent's waveform and meter, the agent's avatar letter, text selection and the bullet dots. **Console Ink** (console-ink) is the action colour on white: the filled button, the selected segmented options and the LLM part ink.
- **Hoplon Gold** (enterprise-gold / showcase-gold): the primary action on navy in Enterprise and Showcase (Call in the band, Hear a sample call in the hero), done steps in the Enterprise stepper, the active theme option in Showcase. On white areas the filled action is navy instead.

### Secondary
- **Session Sky** (studio-lead): the Lead's voice in Studio; lane regions, the Lead swatch and transcript speaker labels.
- **Hoplon Navy** (enterprise-navy, enterprise-navy-deep, showcase-navy, showcase-navy-deep): the nav bar, band and hero gradients, Omar's avatar and waveform, KPI and card headings, the selected Lead outline, and the LLM part ink in both navy themes.
- **Working Blue** (enterprise-blue / showcase-blue): small links and tags inside the navy themes (stage tags, details toggles, Omar speaker label, live meter), never the main action.

### Neutral
- **Horizon Black** (horizon-black): the page, the entry scrim (86% black) and the theme menu (92% black). The world itself sits darker still: **Night Sky** (horizon-night-sky) at the horizon, warming to **Dawn Sky** (horizon-dawn-sky) at the close.
- **Horizon White and Grey** (horizon-white, horizon-grey): white for the words that matter (captions, headings, field values, recap values); grey for everything quieter (field labels, hints, the mode note, the chapter title, the quiet button, recap labels). The Lead's equaliser turns a cool grey.
- **Horizon Line** (horizon-line, 25% white): the only hairline in Horizon; the End call outline, field underlines and the theme menu border.
- **Studio greys** (studio-bg to studio-ink-3): near-black canvas, three panel steps, pure-black seams between panels, and a recessed LCD (studio-lcd) with an inset shadow.
- **Console greys** (console-bg to console-ink-3): warm off-white canvas with white cards and hairline lines. **Console deck** (console-deck to console-deck-ink-3): the black call deck, with a 7% lime glow in its top corner and brighter verdict tones (console-deck-good, -warn, -crit).
- **Enterprise and Showcase blue-greys** (enterprise-bg, showcase-surface-2 and their lines and inks): cool, slightly blue neutrals that belong to the navy.

### Verdict colours
- Horizon has no verdict colours. Its only red is a soft red outline on the error message.
- **Good / Warn / Crit** per measurement theme (studio-good, console-good, enterprise-good, showcase-good and siblings): within 0.9 s, over 0.9 s, over 1.5 s. Light themes pair each with a tinted background (`*-good-bg` etc.). Studio uses bright tones on dark (warn is yellow, `#ffd04d`).
- **End red** (`*-end`): the End call and Stop sample button only (shared `.b-end`).

### Named Rules
**The Black Dominates Rule.** In Horizon black is the material and lime is the light. Lime stays thin (bars, rims, contour lines, a seam, a dot, a word in italics) and the scene stays dark; the bloom is low (strength about 0.4) and the bars are 72% opaque. The theme was toned down once after it read as blinding; a new Horizon element earns lime only as a line or edge.

**The Six Inks Rule.** Each measurement theme defines exactly six part inks (`--p-detect`, `--p-commit`, `--p-tool`, `--p-llm`, `--p-guard`, `--p-tts`) and they always appear in that order: in bars, legends, the Studio wait editor and the Showcase anatomy. Detect and Commit are light-to-mid blues, Tool a violet, LLM the theme's strongest ink (violet in Studio, ink black in Console, navy in Enterprise and Showcase), Guard a yellow or gold, TTS a teal.

**The Verdict Rule.** Green, amber and red say only one thing: how a measured wait compares with 0.9 s and 1.5 s (`tone()` in `web/lib/view.ts`). A dashed green line marks 0.9 s and a dashed red line marks 1.5 s wherever a scale is drawn.

**The One Action Rule.** Each button group has one filled action; its partner is a secondary, outline, ghost or text button. Horizon fills lime (Start the call, Call me again) beside an underlined grey text button. Studio fills amber, Console fills ink black (with a lime icon). Enterprise and Showcase fill gold on navy (band, hero) and navy on white (empty state, try-it section).

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is a per-theme decision. Horizon's depth is real 3D, not CSS: a perspective camera that moves to a new place each chapter (it eases toward the orb while the agent speaks and drifts back while the Lead speaks), a mirror sea that ripples out from the orb with the voice, distance haze into the horizon colour, a low bloom, film grain and a vignette. Its CSS layer is flat: no cards and no drop shadows, only dark text shadows that keep words readable over the scene and two small lime glows. Studio is tonal: panels step from `#111215` to `#1d1f24`, separated by black seams; the only lifts are the recessed LCD (`inset 0 1px 6px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.6)`) and the empty-state card (`0 20px 60px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.5)`). Console is almost flat on white (a 7% ink ring and a soft ambient shadow on cards) and lifts only the black deck (`0 20px 40px -24px rgb(13 15 18 / 0.5)`). Enterprise is layered: two navy-tinted ambient shadows, with the stronger one on KPI tiles and the featured card. Showcase lifts only the product window and the gold button.

## Shapes

Horizon is round and open: pill actions (999px), circles for the orb, the two entry eyes and the speaking dot, and fields drawn as a single underline with no box. The orb's silhouette is its equaliser: 180 thin bars standing out from the rim, mirrored so low frequencies sit at the top and high ones at the bottom. The only rounded rectangles are the theme menu and the error message (10px) and the menu options (6px).

Radius scales with the theme's formality. Studio is tight (7px controls, 6px lane regions, 4px markers, 12px for the one floating card). Console follows hoplonco.com: pill controls, tags and tabs (999px), 12px Lead rows, 16px cards and a 20px deck. Enterprise is generous (12px controls, 14px options, 16px KPI tiles, 18px cards, 20px pills). Showcase is the roundest (pill buttons at 999px, 20px window and transcript card, 14px Lead options, 17px-radius anatomy bar). Part bars always use small radii (3 to 6px, 9px in Showcase rows), with 1px gaps between parts (2 to 3px in larger bars). LLM-made events use a 7px square rotated 45 degrees; code-made events use the same square unrotated.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** keep black dominant in Horizon: lime only on lines, edges, small marks and the one filled action.
- **Do** let the voice drive Horizon: the equaliser reads the real call audio (agent in lime, Lead in cool grey) and the chapter follows the call's stage.
- **Do** keep Horizon's fallbacks working: reduced motion holds the camera still, and with no WebGL a plain night gradient carries the page.
- **Do** keep the six part inks in the order Turn detect, Commit, Tool step, LLM first token, Guard, Voice (TTS) in every bar, legend and list.
- **Do** colour a total by `tone()`: within 0.9 s green, up to 1.5 s amber (yellow in Studio), over 1.5 s red, and say it in words too ("Within target", "Over 0.9 s", "Over 1.5 s").
- **Do** label the sample call as synthetic wherever its numbers show ("Status · synthetic sample", "Sample · synthetic data", "Sample · synthetic numbers", "Sample call · preview").
- **Do** set new values through the theme's variables (`--ac`, `--line`, `--p-*`) so shared blocks keep working in all four measurement themes; in Horizon use the `--hz-*` tokens.
- **Do** keep the theme switch inside each theme's own chrome (behind the small Theme disclosure in Horizon) and keep `?ui=` and the saved choice in step.
- **Do** use the shared SVG icon family (24px grid, 1.75 stroke, round caps).

### Don't:
- **Don't** add background music, chimes or generated sound to any theme or sample; voices only.
- **Don't** add bubble particles, including in Horizon's underwater chapter; keep the motion calm.
- **Don't** fill Horizon with lime: no lime backgrounds, washes, large lime areas or stronger bloom (it was toned down after reading as blinding).
- **Don't** show latency, milliseconds, parts, verdicts or other tech figures in Horizon; the measurement belongs to the other four themes.
- **Don't** reorder, merge or recolour the six parts per screen; a part keeps its ink across the whole theme.
- **Don't** put two filled actions side by side; pair the filled action with an outline, ghost or secondary button.
- **Don't** use drop shadows to separate Studio panels; use value steps and seams.
- **Don't** show a number the call did not measure; unmeasured values show an en dash.
- **Don't** bring back the retired house ledger look (Noto Sans, navy band over a white ledger table).
