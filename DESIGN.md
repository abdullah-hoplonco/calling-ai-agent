# Design System: Talk to Omar (four themes, one call)

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Hear the Call, Then See the Wait"**

One call model wears four complete visual worlds. Studio (the default) is a dark recording session: a transport bar with an LCD clock, stacked lanes for Time, Stages, Lead, Omar, Wait and Events, and an Inspector, Wait editor and Code state below. Console is a light, neutral product console with one blue, a recording card, a transcript with a delay badge on each reply, and a tabbed side panel. Enterprise is a premium workspace: a Hoplon navy band with a gold primary action, a six-step stepper, raised KPI tiles that overlap the band, and soft-shadowed cards. Showcase is a navy landing page where the working call window is the product shot, followed by long editorial sections.

The worlds share no surface, font or radius. They share the measurement. Every theme shows the gap between the Lead's last word and Omar's first sound as one bar split into six parts in a fixed order, and judges totals against the same two targets (0.9 s typical, 1.5 s slow). The theme switch is a segmented control inside each theme's own chrome; the choice is saved per browser (`localStorage` key `omar-ui`) and in the URL (`?ui=studio|console|enterprise|showcase`), with the URL winning on load. The spoken sample call is labelled synthetic in every theme.

Density follows the world: Studio and Console are tight tools on a 13.5px base that fill one viewport (`100dvh`, internal scroll), Enterprise is a roomier 14px page with a 1400px content column, and Showcase is a 16px scrolling page with section padding up to 7rem. The old house look (Noto Sans, navy-band ledger) is retired; the Hoplon logo and name stay.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Four themes, one call model; theme switch saved per browser and via `?ui=`.
- Six part inks per theme, always in the order Turn detect, Commit, Tool step, LLM first token, Guard, Voice (TTS).
- Green, amber and red read as verdicts against 0.9 s and 1.5 s.
- Numbers use tabular, lining figures everywhere (`tnum`, `lnum` on the root of every theme).
- The sample call is spoken with browser voices and labelled synthetic.
- One SVG icon family (24px grid, 1.75 stroke, round caps) sized 16px by default.

## Colors

Each theme owns a complete palette under `.ui-<theme>`, set through the same variable names (`--bg`, `--sf`, `--line`, `--tx`/`--tx2`/`--tx3`, `--ac`, `--good`/`--warn`/`--crit`, `--p-*`), so shared components restyle themselves.

### Primary
- **Session Amber** (studio-omar): Omar's voice in Studio and Studio's accent. It fills Omar's lane regions, the Call button, the selected Lead option and the text-selection tint.
- **Console Blue** (console-blue): the one accent in Console. Primary button, live status pill, current stage, Omar's waveform and avatar, LLM event markers; it is also the Console LLM part ink.
- **Hoplon Gold** (enterprise-gold / showcase-gold): the primary action on navy in Enterprise and Showcase (Call in the band, Hear a sample call in the hero), done steps in the Enterprise stepper, the active theme option in Showcase. On white areas the filled action is navy instead.

### Secondary
- **Session Sky** (studio-lead): the Lead's voice in Studio; lane regions, the Lead swatch and transcript speaker labels.
- **Hoplon Navy** (enterprise-navy, enterprise-navy-deep, showcase-navy, showcase-navy-deep): the nav bar, band and hero gradients, Omar's avatar and waveform, KPI and card headings, the selected Lead outline, and the LLM part ink in both navy themes.
- **Working Blue** (enterprise-blue / showcase-blue): small links and tags inside the navy themes (stage tags, details toggles, Omar speaker label, live meter), never the main action.

### Neutral
- **Studio greys** (studio-bg to studio-ink-3): near-black canvas, three panel steps, pure-black seams between panels, and a recessed LCD (studio-lcd) with an inset shadow.
- **Console greys** (console-bg to console-ink-3): off-white canvas with white cards, hairline lines, and a quiet hover grey.
- **Enterprise and Showcase blue-greys** (enterprise-bg, showcase-surface-2 and their lines and inks): cool, slightly blue neutrals that belong to the navy.

### Verdict colours
- **Good / Warn / Crit** per theme (studio-good, console-good, enterprise-good, showcase-good and siblings): within 0.9 s, over 0.9 s, over 1.5 s. Light themes pair each with a tinted background (`*-good-bg` etc.). Studio uses bright tones on dark (warn is yellow, `#ffd04d`).
- **End red** (`*-end`): the End call and Stop sample button only (shared `.b-end`).

### Named Rules
**The Six Inks Rule.** Each theme defines exactly six part inks (`--p-detect`, `--p-commit`, `--p-tool`, `--p-llm`, `--p-guard`, `--p-tts`) and they always appear in that order: in bars, legends, the Studio wait editor and the Showcase anatomy. Detect and Commit are light-to-mid blues, Tool a violet, LLM the theme's strongest ink (violet in Studio, the accent blue in Console, navy in Enterprise and Showcase), Guard a yellow or gold, TTS a teal.

**The Verdict Rule.** Green, amber and red say only one thing: how a measured wait compares with 0.9 s and 1.5 s (`tone()` in `web/lib/view.ts`). A dashed green line marks 0.9 s and a dashed red line marks 1.5 s wherever a scale is drawn.

**The One Action Rule.** Each button group has one filled action; its partner is a secondary, outline or ghost button. Studio fills amber, Console fills blue. Enterprise and Showcase fill gold on navy (band, hero) and navy on white (empty state, try-it section).

## Typography

**Studio:** Geist (body, labels), Geist Mono (timecode, readouts, axis ticks).
**Console:** Inter with `cv11`.
**Enterprise:** Manrope.
**Showcase:** Bricolage Grotesque (display, at optical size 96) over Figtree (body).

**Character:** Each theme carries one face (two in Studio and Showcase), loaded with `next/font`; only Studio's faces preload. Studio reads like hardware: small tracked uppercase panel labels and mono numbers. Console is a quiet product sans with medium weights (550 to 650). Enterprise is heavy and confident (800 for titles and figures). Showcase is editorial, with a tight, large grotesque.

### Hierarchy
- **Studio title** (750, 1.375rem): the empty-state card heading only; Studio has no page headline.
- **Studio label** (700, 0.625rem, 0.12em, uppercase): lane headers, panel headers and LCD field labels, like the printed labels on a console.
- **Studio timecode** (Geist Mono 500, 1.375rem): the LCD position clock; **Studio readout** (Geist Mono 600, 2.25rem): the selected reply's total wait.
- **Console title** (650, 1.0625rem): the call header; **Console metric** (650, 1.25 to 1.375rem): stat strip and KPI figures; **Console label** (500, 0.75rem): stat captions and section names, sentence case.
- **Enterprise display** (800, clamp 1.75 to 2.375rem): the band title; **Enterprise metric** (800, 2rem, navy): KPI tiles; **Enterprise headline** (800, 1.0625rem, navy): card titles.
- **Showcase display** (700, clamp 2.6 to 4.9rem, line height 0.98, max 13ch, balanced): the hero; **Showcase headline** (700, clamp 2 to 3.25rem): section titles; **Showcase lede** (clamp 1.05 to 1.25rem, max 50ch).
- **Body** per theme: 13.5px (Studio, Console), 14px (Enterprise), 16px (Showcase); spoken lines max 70 to 76ch.

### Named Rules
**The Tabular Figures Rule.** Every theme sets `font-feature-settings: "tnum", "lnum"` at its root, so milliseconds line up in lists, tables and LCD fields.

**The Sentence Case Rule.** Outside Studio's hardware labels, labels and section names are sentence case with no tracking.

## Layout

- **Studio** is a fixed application frame (`100dvh`, no page scroll): transport bar on top; the arrangement with a 224px sticky track-header column and a horizontally scrolling timeline (a faint grid line every 300px); a bottom dock of three panels (`1fr / 1.3fr / minmax(280px, 0.8fr)`, height `clamp(300px, 42vh, 430px)`). With no call, a centred Lead-picker card (max 560px) sits over a dimmed arrangement. Below 900px the frame becomes a scrolling page: the LCD takes a full row, the header column narrows to 112px and the panels stack.
- **Console** is a 248px sidebar plus main column (`100dvh`). The main column has a 56px top bar and a content grid of `1fr 380px` with 1rem gaps. Below 1180px the side panel drops under the main column; below 820px the sidebar moves below the main content.
- **Enterprise** is a scrolling page: nav, then a navy band whose bottom padding (5.5rem) lets the KPI row (4 columns, 1rem gap) overlap it by 3.75rem; then a `1fr 400px` grid with 1.25rem gaps. Content is capped at 1400px with `clamp(1rem, 4vw, 3rem)` gutters. KPIs go to 2 columns at 1180px; the stepper goes to 3 columns at 760px.
- **Showcase** is a long landing page: sticky blurred nav, a two-column hero (`1fr 1.05fr`), then sections for numbers, the anatomy of one wait (3-column legend), rules (2 columns) and the try-it area, with section padding `clamp(3.5rem, 8vw, 7rem)`. Columns collapse at 1080px; the legend goes to one column at 640px.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is a per-theme decision. Studio is tonal: panels step from `#111215` to `#1d1f24`, separated by black seams; the only lifts are the recessed LCD (`inset 0 1px 6px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.6)`) and the empty-state card (`0 20px 60px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.5)`). Console is almost flat: one hairline shadow on cards and buttons. Enterprise is layered: two navy-tinted ambient shadows, with the stronger one on KPI tiles and the featured card. Showcase lifts only the product window and the gold button.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Console hairline** (`box-shadow: 0 1px 2px rgb(16 24 40 / 0.05)`): cards, buttons, the selected sidebar Lead.
- **Enterprise shadow-1** (`box-shadow: 0 1px 2px rgb(11 46 107 / 0.06), 0 6px 20px -4px rgb(11 46 107 / 0.08)`): standard cards.
- **Enterprise shadow-2** (`box-shadow: 0 2px 4px rgb(11 46 107 / 0.06), 0 18px 40px -12px rgb(11 46 107 / 0.18)`): KPI tiles and the featured card.
- **Gold glow** (`0 6px 18px -6px rgb(242 194 48 / 0.6)` Enterprise; `0 10px 30px -10px rgb(255 201 51 / 0.7)` Showcase): under the gold action only.
- **Product window** (`box-shadow: 0 40px 80px -30px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.55), 0 0 0 1px rgb(255 255 255 / 0.06)`): the Showcase call window on navy.

### Named Rules
**The Tonal Studio Rule.** Studio separates surfaces with value steps and black seams, not drop shadows.

## Shapes

Radius scales with the theme's formality. Studio is tight (7px controls, 6px lane regions, 4px markers, 12px for the one floating card). Console is moderate (7px controls, 9px tiles, 11px cards). Enterprise is generous (12px controls, 14px options, 16px KPI tiles, 18px cards, 20px pills). Showcase is the roundest (pill buttons at 999px, 20px window and transcript card, 14px Lead options, 17px-radius anatomy bar). Part bars always use small radii (3 to 6px, 9px in Showcase rows), with 1px gaps between parts (2 to 3px in larger bars). LLM-made events use a 7px square rotated 45 degrees; code-made events use the same square unrotated.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Shared base:** inline-flex, 0.5rem icon gap, weight 650, 150ms colour transitions, 1px press-down on `:active`. End call and Stop sample are always the theme's end red with white text.
- **Studio:** 36px amber Call (`#1c0d00` text, weight 750); grey secondary (studio-button-2 with line-2 border); 36px square mute.
- **Console:** 34px white buttons with line-2 border and hairline shadow; blue primary; 34px mute.
- **Enterprise:** 44px, 12px radius. Gold in the band, ghost (white 8% fill, 22% border) beside it; navy and outline variants on white cards.
- **Showcase:** 52px pills (48px under 640px), 1rem 750 text, 18px icons. Gold primary, white line button on navy, navy and outline on white.
- **Focus:** a 2px outline in the theme accent with 2px offset (shared).

### Theme switch
- A segmented radio group (`.ts`) with 3px inner padding. The selected option is a raised chip: light chip on black in Studio, white in Console (2 by 2 grid in the sidebar), white on the navy nav in Enterprise, gold pill in Showcase.

### Chips and badges
- Pills (20px radius) for status, stage and verdict in Enterprise and Showcase; 5px radius tags in Console; Studio verdict pills on a 14% tint of the verdict colour. Rule chips mark LLM-made items with an accent-tinted border.

### Cards / Containers
- **Studio panels:** flush docked panels with a 34px header bar.
- **Console card:** white, 1px line, 11px radius, hairline shadow.
- **Enterprise card:** white, 18px radius, shadow-1, 1.25rem by 1.35rem padding, header with a bottom rule and 800-weight navy title; featured card has a white-to-`#f7f9ff` gradient and shadow-2.
- **Showcase window:** white, 20px radius, product-window shadow, title bar with a pulsing red live dot.

### Lead picker
- Radio cards with a 32px initials avatar, name, two-line message clamp and a note. Selected state: accent border plus 1px accent ring (amber in Studio, navy in Enterprise and Showcase). Console instead lists Leads as sidebar rows; the selected row is a white raised row.

- **Parts list:** swatch, part name, ms and percent, closed by a "Total wait" row over a rule.
- **Studio wait editor:** 54px blocks per part with mono values, dashed green 0.9 s and red 1.5 s lines, and a mono axis.
- **Console transcript:** each Omar reply carries a verdict badge and a 100px, 5px-high mini bar.
- **Enterprise wait chip:** a dashed-border button under each reply with a 160px bar and the total in its verdict colour.
- **Showcase anatomy:** a 34px rounded bar with a 3-column legend of 14px swatches.

### Navigation
- Studio: the transport is the navigation. Console: sidebar plus 56px breadcrumb bar. Enterprise: deep-navy nav bar over the band with a six-dot stepper (done gold, current white with a 6px halo). Showcase: sticky nav at 92% deep navy with `blur(10px)` and on-navy links.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** keep the six part inks in the order Turn detect, Commit, Tool step, LLM first token, Guard, Voice (TTS) in every bar, legend and list.
- **Do** colour a total by `tone()`: within 0.9 s green, up to 1.5 s amber (yellow in Studio), over 1.5 s red, and say it in words too ("Within target", "Over 0.9 s", "Over 1.5 s").
- **Do** label the sample call as synthetic wherever its numbers show ("Status · synthetic sample", "Sample · synthetic data", "Sample · synthetic numbers", "Sample call · preview").
- **Do** set new values through the theme's variables (`--ac`, `--line`, `--p-*`) so shared blocks keep working in all four themes.
- **Do** keep the theme switch inside each theme's own chrome and keep `?ui=` and the saved choice in step.
- **Do** use the shared SVG icon family (24px grid, 1.75 stroke, round caps).

### Don't:
- **Don't** reorder, merge or recolour the six parts per screen; a part keeps its ink across the whole theme.
- **Don't** put two filled actions side by side; pair the filled action with an outline, ghost or secondary button.
- **Don't** use drop shadows to separate Studio panels; use value steps and seams.
- **Don't** show a number the call did not measure; unmeasured values show an en dash.
- **Don't** bring back the retired house ledger look (Noto Sans, navy band over a white ledger table).
