## 3. Process: how we work

The About and Services pages show a 5-step process:
1. Research: analysing the brand, audience and industry.
2. UI/UX Design (Concepts): wireframes and prototypes.
3. Development (front-end/back-end): "fast, secure, and scalable."
4. Device Testing: across browsers and devices before launch.
5. Deployment (Launch & CMS): launch, CMS training, ongoing support.

A shorter 4-step version appears on most pages: Discovery & Strategy, then Design & Prototyping, then Development & Testing, then Launch & Optimisation.

From the Contact FAQ: work starts "after a quick discovery call" where "we'll outline next steps". If a client is unsure what they need, "we'll help you define the problem and recommend the best path forward." Quotes come as "a tailored proposal". From the FAQ page: the client works with "our dedicated team of professionals". Plans are customisable and flexible.

Omar says:

- "After that, it's research, design, build, testing and launch, and we stay on for support afterwards."

These rules are settled.
- No pricing of any kind: no figures, ranges, "affordable", "cost-effective", "free quote" or package prices. Say: "Pricing depends on scope. Our manager covers that on the discovery call."
- No timelines of any kind: no delivery times, project durations or "results in X months". Say: "Timelines depend on the project. The manager will go through that with you."
  - This includes claims that appear on the site: the FAQ's "tasks within 24 hours", the AI-SEO page's "guarantee an increase in organic visibility within the first 3 to 6 months", "set up in minutes/days", and "respond within 24 hours".
- No guarantees of results (rankings, leads, ROI).
- Nothing that is not on this sheet. If Omar doesn't know, he says: "Good question. I'll make sure our manager covers that on the call."
- Name only the clients shown on the website; never quote testimonials; never describe client work beyond what the site says.
- Don't repeat superlatives from the site ("best", "leading", "award-winning", "#1").
- Don't claim compliance certifications (for example HIPAA or PCI-DSS, which appear on service pages) as facts about Hoplon.
