# C-suite risk register: Outbound Calling Agent

Every risk the C-suite must see before approving go-live. Each row is **accepted**, **mitigated** or **open**. Sources are the research files and tickets in this folder. Legal points are research findings, not legal advice; the legal team confirms them (tickets 18, 20, 22).

Fine tiers are from Cabinet Resolution 57/2024: AED 10k-150k per violation, escalating tiers, plus possible suspension (7-90 days), licence cancellation or number removal. A repeat within 6 months allows the maximum.

## Legal and compliance (owner: legal team)

| # | Risk | Impact | Status | Mitigation / next step | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | **Foreign caller ID.** Twilio can only present a foreign number on UAE calls. Res 56/2024 Art. 4(3), 4(13) require a local e&/du number registered to our trade licence. | Every marketing call is a violation until fixed; foreign numbers may also be blocked or ignored, lowering answer rates. | Open | Legal team gets an e&/du trunk connected to Twilio via BYOC (ticket 22). No engineering change needed. **Blocks go-live.** | research/02, research/01 |
| R2 | **No prior approval** from the Competent Authority (likely the licensing body, e.g. Dubai DET) for telemarketing. | Top fine tier (AED 150k). | Open | Legal team applies (ticket 18). **Blocks go-live.** | research/01 |
| R3 | **Backlog Leads lack explicit consent.** Existing form submissions had no checkbox covering calls, recording and overseas processing. | PDPL breach and possible Res 56 complaints; reputational risk. | **Accepted by owner** (2026-09-26): backlog treated as callable. | Present explicitly to the C-suite. The new form checkbox and evidence capture (ticket 20) fix it for future Leads. | research/01 |
| R4 | **Do Not Call Registry (DNCR) checks** not yet in place; there is no stated exception for Leads who asked to be called. | Top fine tier (AED 150k) per DNCR call. | Open | Legal team confirms the check procedure (ticket 18); engineering adds a pre-dial DNCR gate once it is known. | research/01 |
| R5 | **Recording consent.** Recording is mandatory, but recording without consent is a separate crime (Penal Code Art. 431, Cybercrime Law Art. 44). It is unclear whether the call announcement alone counts as consent. | Criminal exposure. | Mitigated in design; open legally | Recording notice at the start of every call (built into the opening); the call ends if the Lead objects; the form checkbox covers recording. Legal team confirms (ticket 18). | research/01 |
| R6 | **Cross-border data transfer.** Voice and transcripts go to Twilio (Ireland) and STT/LLM/TTS vendors (EU/US). The PDPL needs explicit consent plus data processing agreements. | PDPL breach. | Open | Consent wording names overseas processors (ticket 20); legal team signs DPAs with each vendor. | research/01, ticket 17 |
| R7 | **AI disclosure.** The agent doesn't volunteer that it is an AI (lawful outside DIFC per research). DIFC licensing status is unconfirmed, and the new Federal Authority for AI and Data (announced 14 Jun 2026) may issue new rules. | Rule change could force up-front disclosure. | Mitigated in design | The agent never denies being an AI and says yes when asked (deception fine AED 25k-75k avoided). Legal team confirms DIFC status (ticket 18). Disclosure wording is config, so switching is cheap. | research/01, ADR 0001 |
| R8 | **PDPL implementing regulations not yet issued** (as of Jun 2026). | Requirements may change after launch. | Open | Legal team monitors; keep retention and consent rules configurable. | research/01 |
| R9 | **Warm-lead exemption uncertain.** Calls "at the request of the Consumer" are excluded from "Unwanted" calls, but not clearly from the call rules. | If misread, calls may breach rules we assumed didn't apply. | Mitigated in design | The design follows every Res 56 control regardless: 09:00-18:00 window, retry limits, opening order, recording. | research/01 |
| R10 | **LLM data to China.** DeepSeek's first-party API is China-hosted; Lead conversations sent there would add a cross-border transfer outside the UAE/EU vendors named in consent. | PDPL breach; reputational risk. | Mitigated in design | Use DeepSeek V4.1 Flash only via US/EU hosts (ticket 14); name the host in the consent and privacy notice. | ticket 14 |

## Product and technical (owner: engineering)

| # | Risk | Impact | Status | Mitigation / next step | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | **Accent accuracy unknown.** There is no independent STT data for Gulf Arab, South Asian or Filipino English on phone audio. | Misheard names, emails and times lead to bad bookings and a poor Lead experience. | **Accepted by owner** (2026-09-30) | No pre-launch accent test; measured on soft-launch calls through the eval pipeline; email read-back on every call. | research/03 |
| T2 | **Latency.** Hosting in the EU next to Twilio adds ~100-150ms each way; the realistic median reply time is ~1.1s. | Conversation feels slow, which lowers conversion. | Open | Per-stage latency budget and measurement on test calls (ticket 15); faster-model option (ticket 14). | research/02, research/03 |
| T3 | **LLM claims a booking that didn't happen.** Every tested model sometimes does this. | A Lead expects a meeting that doesn't exist. | Mitigated in design | Code confirms the calendar booking before the agent may say "booked" (ADR 0001). | research/03 |
| T4 | **Voicemail detection accuracy** in the UAE is unknown (Twilio publishes US/Canada figures only). | Voicemails treated as live calls (wasted attempts) or live Leads hung up on. | Open | Our own detector plus Twilio's signal as a hint (ticket 17); tuned on test calls. | research/02 |
| T5 | **Calendar access expiry.** Google OAuth tokens can be revoked or expire (7 days if the app is left in Testing mode). | Bookings fail silently. | Mitigated in design | Publish the OAuth app to Production; alert on token failure; fall back to the booking link. | research/04 |
| T8 | **LLM latency unproven.** One reported DeepSeek V4.1 Flash time-to-first-token (~1.37 s) would miss the 1.2 s target. | Slow replies hurt conversion. | Open | Host benchmark (ticket 23); Haiku 4.5 backup via the adapter. | ticket 14 |
| T7 | **Website team dependency.** The consent checkbox, Service dropdown and evidence capture need the separate website team (ticket 20). | Without them, new Leads keep the backlog's consent gap (R3) and the service has to be guessed. | Open | Change request handed over; ingestion copes with both formats. | ticket 20 |
| T6 | **Running cost (estimates with a 30% safety margin, 2026-10-03).** Usage ≈ $0.44 per connected minute (phone ≈ $0.39); fixed ≈ $825–1,145/month (GPU, Azure platform, LiveKit, nightly evals). All-in ≈ $1.00–1.20/min at 1,500 min/month, falling to ≈ $0.52–0.56/min at 10,000. Real costs are reported after M2 test calls. | Budget visibility. | Mitigated in design | A per-call cost ledger shown on the dashboard. | research/02, research/03 |
