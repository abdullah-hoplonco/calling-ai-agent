# Calling AI Agent

An AI voice agent that places outbound phone calls to warm leads for a UAE web/mobile development and digital marketing agency, qualifies them, and books discovery calls.

## Language

**Lead**:
A person who has already expressed interest in the agency's services through an inbound channel and has not yet been qualified.
_Avoid_: Prospect, contact, customer

**Calling Agent**:
The AI system that places phone calls to Leads and converses with them by voice.
_Avoid_: Bot, dialer, assistant

**Discovery Call**:
A meeting between a Qualified Lead and the Manager, booked by the Calling Agent on the Manager's Google Calendar. Budget and timeline are discussed here, never on the Connected Call.
_Avoid_: Meeting, appointment, demo

**Submission**:
One filled-in website form; a Lead can have several.
_Avoid_: Form entry, enquiry, lead (for a single form fill)

**Call Attempt**:
Any outbound dial from the Calling Agent to a Lead, answered or not.
_Avoid_: Call (when it's unclear whether anyone answered), dial

**Connected Call**:
A Call Attempt where the Lead answered and a live conversation took place, aimed at detecting Intent to Buy and booking a Discovery Call.
_Avoid_: Qualification Call, sales call, cold call

**Intent to Buy**:
A Lead's expressed willingness to pursue the agency's services, shown by saying so, asking about price, or asking to speak to someone; the sole qualification criterion for booking a Discovery Call.
_Avoid_: Qualification score, BANT

**Qualified Lead**:
A Lead who showed Intent to Buy on a Connected Call.

**Parked Lead**:
A Lead who did not show Intent to Buy but did not reject contact, set aside for re-nurturing or a later decision.
_Avoid_: Disqualified, dead lead

**Opted-out Lead**:
A Lead who rejected contact ("not interested", "don't call me"); never called again.
_Avoid_: Rejected, unsubscribed, DNC lead

**Unreachable Lead**:
A Lead who did not answer any call attempt in the permitted attempt sequence.
_Avoid_: Dead number, no-show

**Manager**:
The human the Calling Agent refers to when a Lead needs a person; the host of Discovery Calls.
_Avoid_: Human agent, operator, "a human"
