1What we will build

Many people fill in the form on hoplonco.com. Today, we do not call all of them quickly.

Omar is an AI Calling Agent. He calls each Lead by phone, in English. He:

  • Gives the information that the Lead asked for.
  • Finds out if the Lead wants to buy (Intent to Buy).
  • Books a 30-minute Discovery Call with our Manager on Google Calendar.

Omar calls a new Lead some minutes after the form arrives. He also calls the old Leads in our Excel file.

Omar never talks about price or timeline. The Manager does that.

AI Calling Agent: risks, plan and decisions
⚠ Two items stop go-live. The legal team must close them.
  • R1, caller number: UAE law requires a local e& or du number, registered to our trade licence. Today, our phone provider can show only a foreign number.
  • R2, approval: marketing calls need prior approval from the authority. The fine for calls without approval is up to AED 150,000.

8Plan: six steps

9Decisions we need from you

  1. Approve the estimated budget: about $0.44 for each call minute, plus about $825–1,145 each month. Real costs follow after the first tests.
  2. Tell the legal team to close R1 and R2. Also ask them to confirm R4–R6.
  3. Accept risk R3: we call old Leads without a recorded consent box.
  4. Approve the soft launch: 10 Leads, then 25 Leads, with a review of each call.

10Later: version 2

  • Move all parts to Azure UAE North, with a local e&/du line. Reply time goes to about 0.5–0.7 s.
  • All call data stays in the UAE.
  • Add CRM updates, Arabic, and inbound calls.
  • Sell the Calling Agent to our clients as a product.
Our promise for safety: Omar says that he is an AI if a Lead asks. He never says "booked" before the calendar confirms. He obeys the legal call hours and call limits. He stops at once on "do not call me".
